> [!info]+ Assessment in the archaeological report of whether the sample dating result corresponds with the archaeological feature. ‘N’ implies that the dating in the report does not agree with the dating of the site. It doesn't mean that the dating is technically wrong. The age determination may very well be correct. (Contamination, random wrong choice of dated material. Misinterpretation of the context are some reasons.)
